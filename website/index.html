How it works

every hand-off is recorded, so nothing goes missing

In the shop

stock moves from the supplier to the door
  1. 01
    🚚

    Supplier

    Delivers goods with an invoice. Snap it and the items fill in by themselves.

  2. 02
    📦

    Store manager

    Counts what arrived into the store room, notes short deliveries and expiry dates.

  3. 03
    🗂️

    Shelf manager

    Confirms each handover onto the shelves. Stock only moves once it's signed for.

  4. 04
    🧺

    Counter

    Customers pick from full, priced shelves. Low shelves raise a refill alert.

  5. 05
    🧾

    Cashier

    Scans, weighs and takes cash, transfer, card or split. Prints or WhatsApps a QR receipt.

  6. 06
    🛡️

    Security

    Scans the receipt at the door and ticks every item. A receipt works only once.

Online

customers order from your own shop page
  1. 01
    🔐

    Log in

    Customers open your shop link and sign in, or check out as a guest.

  2. 02
    🛍️

    Browse products

    Your live stock, prices and photos, in your shop's colours and logo.

  3. 03
    ✅

    Select

    Add to cart, by the piece or by weight, and choose delivery or pickup.

  4. 04
    💳

    Pay their way

    Whatever suits the customer:

    OnlineOn deliveryOn creditOn pickup
  5. 05
    🎉

    Received

    The order lands in your dashboard, gets packed and delivered, and the customer can track it.

Features

the essentials, nothing extra
01

Fast checkout

Scan or search, weigh items sold by the kilo, take cash, transfer, card or split payment, and send the receipt by print or WhatsApp.

02

Works with no signal

Sales and stock changes save on the device and sync when the internet is back. Shop computers keep each other up to date over the shop Wi-Fi.

03

Staff can't sell from home

Switch on "in-store only" and cashiers can only sell or move stock from inside the shop. You get an alert if anyone tries from outside.

04

Your own online shop

Your shop gets its own page with your logo and colours. Customers order for delivery or pickup, and orders land straight in your dashboard.

05

Smart reordering

Reorder suggestions from what actually sells, turned into a purchase order in one tap.

06

Credit sales, guaranteed

Let trusted customers buy now and pay later, backed by their bank, cooperative or pension fund after a proper KYC check.
