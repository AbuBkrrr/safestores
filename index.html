What it actually does

no fluff — this is the whole job
01

Stock, start to finish

Record arrivals, move stock to the shelf, count it, and track batches by expiry — with FIFO markdowns as dates approach.

02

POS checkout

Scan or add items, take payment, print a receipt. Split checkout mode lets one person scan while another handles cash.

03

Smart replenishment

Reorder suggestions from real sales velocity, not guesswork. Turns into a purchase order in one click.

04

Works with no signal

Every write queues locally and replays once you're back online. Reads fall back to the last successful load.

05

Public storefront

Opt in and shoppers can browse and order — guest checkout included. Orders land straight in your dashboard.

06

Built for your whole team

Admin, manager, cashier, stock staff, and external auditors — each sees exactly what their role needs.
